# Quickstart

**English** · [中文版](../zh-CN/quickstart.md)

How to get from a fresh clone to a coding agent answering you in chat. Outline
only — the steps are filled in as P1 lands the WeChat entry and managed
sessions.

## Install

Binary download, `go install`, and building from source. Where Ferry keeps its
data (`FERRY_HOME`, defaulting to `~/.ferry`).

## Log in

Scanning the login QR code from the terminal, what a confirmed login stores, and
how to tell that the platform session has expired. Lands in P1.

## Pair your account

Turning your own chat account into the operator of the daemon, and what an
unpaired stranger sees instead. Lands in P1.

## Your first session

Choosing an agent preset and a working directory, sending the first task, and
reading the streamed reply. Lands in P1.

## Where to go next

Pointers to the WeChat entry, agent and handoff pages once they carry content.
